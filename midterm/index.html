Life of a Puppy

Puppies have way too much energy, get distracted by literally everything, and grow super fast, but they're honestly the best companions to have around. In their first few months, they will spend most of their time Running around, figuring out how the world works, and learning social cues just by playing with each other. Giving them some chew toys, keeping a normal routine, and playing with them helps them turn into great dogs.

Taking care of a puppy means you actually have to stick to routine with a feeding schedule, making sure they get enough sleep, and start train them early. Once they get a bit bigger, keeping up with daily walks, playtime, and regular vet trips is pretty much all it takes to keep them happy and healthy.
