About Me

My name is Alivia Butterly. I am a student at Algonquin College studying Interactive Media Design. I enjoy being creative and learning new things, especially when I can use my own knowledge and incorporate it to help me learn new ideas in different projects. I am intersted in web design and photography so I am happy that this program allows me to learn all of the above media. Also, it allows me to be creative while also learning useful skills. I am excited to continue developing my skills and gaining more experience throughout my time at Algonquin College.

In the next four years, I see myself having a career in photography or even web design or something that allows me to be creative and use my skills I have learned throughout my time in this program. I want to keep learning and improving my skills so I can become more confident in the work that I create. I hope to have a careerthat I can be creative, work on interesting projects, and continue growing my skills.

Outside of school, I enjoy taking sports photos, spending time with my friends and family, and being creative. I enjoy photography because I like being able to capture special moments and memories that people can look back on. In high school last year I took part in a yearbook class and I helped design a number of pages for the yearbook, as well as take photos of different school events to add in. I liked being able to work with photos and different designs to create something that people can look back on and remember. Yearbook class helped me realize that I really do like to do media and design work so I realized I actually want to pursue a career in this field. I am excited to continue learning and developing my skills in this program and see where it takes me.

I am currently in my 1st year of the IMD program at Algonquin College. I am excited to continue learning and developing my skills in this program and see where it takes me.
