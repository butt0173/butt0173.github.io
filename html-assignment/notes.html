5 HTML Tags I have learned

Header

The header tag holds the introduction to the content at the top of a page, like titles, logos, or main navigation.

Nav

The nav tag groups together navigation links so users can easily move between different pages on a site.

Article

The article tag holds a self contained block of content that can stand on its own, like a image section or a blog post.

Section

The section tag breaks content into distinct parts, making it easier to organize and style.

Footer

The footer tag sits at the bottom of a page or section and contains info like copyright details, author info, or other links.
